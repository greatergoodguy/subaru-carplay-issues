Join owners seeking legal action against Subaru

If your 2019–2023 Subaru's CarPlay, USB, Bluetooth, or screen keeps failing and Subaru hasn't fixed it, sign up. We're gathering affected owners and their evidence so consumer-protection law firms can evaluate a class action or group claim. One owner is easy to dismiss. Hundreds with documented failures are not.

Sign up now About a minute. No Google account needed.

How it works

1

Sign up

Tell us your car, what's happening, and what the dealer did. It takes about a minute.

2

We build the case file

Sign-ups are combined with the 282 public NHTSA complaints and Subaru's own bulletins into one record of the defect and the failed repairs.

3

Law firms review it

With your permission, your details go to consumer-protection law firms that handle auto-defect class actions. If a firm takes the case, it contacts you directly.

What helps a case

Start collecting these now, even before anything is filed:

  • Repair orders that mention the problem, even ones that say "could not duplicate"
  • Videos of the failure happening
  • Purchase or lease paperwork showing the delivery date
  • Receipts for anything you paid: diagnostics, parts, cables
  • Dates and mileage of each dealer visit

Important

No lawsuit has been filed yet. This site is run by a Subaru owner, not a law firm. Signing up is free. It doesn't make you anyone's client or a member of any class, and it doesn't commit you to anything.
