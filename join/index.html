Owners are stronger together

If your 2019–2023 Subaru's CarPlay, USB, Bluetooth, or screen keeps failing, add yourself to the list. One complaint is easy to dismiss. Hundreds of documented owners are not, whether the goal is getting Subaru to fix cars or talking with a consumer-protection law firm about group action.

What happens when you join

  1. You get occasional updates about this issue: new bulletins, repair outcomes, and next steps. No spam, and you can leave anytime.
  2. If enough owners join, the list may be used to approach consumer-protection law firms about whether a group case makes sense.
  3. Your details go to a law firm only if you say so. The form has a separate checkbox for that.

This site is run by a Subaru owner, not a law firm. Joining doesn't make you part of any lawsuit, and nothing here is legal advice. Your email is kept in a private sheet, never published, and never sold.
