Subaru's record

What Subaru has acknowledged, and when

Subaru's own technical service bulletins describe the same failures owners report: CarPlay dropping, USB connections cutting out, and head units failing. Here's the timeline, with links to the documents Subaru filed with NHTSA.

Subaru's own bulletinsLawsuits and complaints

2018 – 2020

Earlier class action over Starlink head units

Udeen v. Subaru of America (D.N.J. 1:18-cv-17334) covered 2017–2018 vehicles with the Harman Gen 3.0 head unit. It settled in 2020 with an extended warranty for that earlier generation.

January 2019

Owners of 2019+ models begin filing complaints

The first complaint in this directory about a 2019–2023 head unit was filed with NHTSA in January 2019.

April 2023

New class action covers 2019–2023 models

Cilluffo v. Subaru of America (D.N.J. 1:23-cv-01897) alleged Starlink infotainment defects in 2019–2023 Outback, Legacy, Forester, Crosstrek, and WRX models, including phone-connection failures.

October 2023

Subaru publishes a USB fix for intermittent CarPlay loss

TSB 15-314-23 describes intermittent loss of CarPlay and Android Auto through the USB port on Gen 3.1 head units, and introduces a redesigned USB terminal. It is officially listed for 2019 models only.

Read TSB 15-314-23 (PDF)
March 2024

Court lets most claims proceed

The court denied most of Subaru's motion to dismiss in Cilluffo.

January 2025

Subaru extends the head unit warranty to 8 years / 150,000 miles

TSB 15-322-25 covers head unit failures causing CarPlay/Android Auto, Bluetooth, navigation, or touchscreen to stop working, black screens, lost audio, and reboots, and notes these issues may occur intermittently. Repairs require a dealer to confirm the head unit is the cause.

Read TSB 15-322-25R (PDF)
February 2025

Class action ends without a class settlement

The Cilluffo named plaintiffs' individual claims were dismissed after a private settlement. No class was certified, and there is no claim process for other owners.

Since January 2025

Owners keep reporting failures: 35 more complaints

35 complaints in this directory were filed after the warranty extension was announced, 7 of them involving CarPlay, Android Auto, or USB.

The bulletins

Technical service bulletins are instructions Subaru issues to its dealers. These are the public copies filed with NHTSA.

TSB 15-322-25R: head unit warranty extension (Jan 2025)

Extends coverage on Gen 3.1 and CP1 head units to 8 years / 150,000 miles for failures causing a black or blank screen, loss of audio, repeated rebooting, or inability to use CarPlay/Android Auto, navigation, Bluetooth, or the touchscreen. Repair path: software update first, then head unit replacement if the fault remains. Listed vehicles:

Forester 2019–23Impreza 2019–23Crosstrek 2019–23WRX / STI 2019–23Ascent 2019–22Outback 2019–22Legacy 2019–22

Read the bulletin (PDF)

TSB 15-314-23: redesigned USB terminal (Oct 2023)

Addresses intermittent communication loss with CarPlay and Android Auto through the USB port on Gen 3.1 Harman head units, with a new USB terminal and adapter cord. Officially listed for 2019 models.

Read the bulletin (PDF)

TSB 15-236-18R: Gen 3.1 head unit software (rev. May 2023)

The software reprogramming procedure for Harman Gen 3.1 head units, including 2019–2023 Forester. Under the warranty extension, this is the first repair step before a replacement.

Read the bulletin (PDF)

Why this site exists

It was started by a 2023 Forester owner whose wired CarPlay disconnects on its own while driving. The dealer couldn't reproduce it during service visits, even after seeing video of it. Many owners in this directory describe the same thing: an intermittent failure, "could not duplicate," and advice to try another cable or a software update. Each report is easy to dismiss on its own. Together they are a pattern, and this site keeps that record public.
